Night Archive — a top-down 2D stealth game. You are a night courier slipping through a guarded archive: lift 3 intel files, dodge four patrolling guards and their vision cones, hide behind crates and in shadow patches, and reach the green exit unseen. Guards grow suspicious, then chase, then search — but a detection meter that drains when you break line of sight always gives you a chance to recover. Synthesised footsteps tell you where the guards are.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, P to pause, Space to start.
